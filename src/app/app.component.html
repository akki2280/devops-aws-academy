How we teach

Each session is about 2 hours: a short concept briefing, then guided labs, then a break-it-and-fix-it challenge. The design target is roughly 25% theory and 75% hands-on work, with weekly assessments and a readiness checkpoint at the end of every phase.

Prerequisites: a laptop with 8 GB RAM (16 GB preferred), a basic comfort with computers, and an AWS and GitHub account (setup help provided). No prior DevOps knowledge is required.
